01 / Requirement

Which programs are
quietly slipping?

Americold's revenue lives in long-running storage programs. When a customer stops using the space the way they contracted, the program is on its way out - and no one saw it coming.

"By the time a program showed up in the churn report, we'd already lost it. We needed to see the drift, not the death."- Commercial stakeholder, Americold

Every program carries a profile - expected pallet positions, inbound volume, turns, case-pick. When actual activity drifts below that profile, or a customer's rates fall behind market, the account is quietly eroding. Spread across thousands of programs and dozens of sites, that erosion was invisible until revenue simply stopped.

The brief: one place where commercial teams read adherence and risk across the whole portfolio, drill into any program, and act before it becomes a lost program.

02 / Research

Two questions
behind every account

I worked with commercial and analytics teams to separate the two things that actually predict a lost program - and to make a dense dataset legible.

6

Profile metrics

Pallet positions, peak positions, inbound pallets, inbound cases, turns and case-pick.

4

Time windows

Trailing 6, 12, 26 and 52-week averages against the contracted assumption.

4

Risk signals

Storage rate, handling rate, occupancy trend and perfect-order - beyond adherence alone.

The clarifying insight: adherence and risk are two different questions. Adherence asks "are they using what they committed?" - a leading indicator of drift. Risk asks "are they profitable and staying?" - rates versus market, occupancy trend, perfect-order performance.

So the product splits into CPA and CRA, tied together by a summary dashboard - and every dense table earns an AI summary that reads it in plain language.

Adherence is the leading signal

Drift below profile shows up weeks before revenue does - it's the earliest warning of a slipping account.

Risk is more than adherence

Rates versus market, occupancy trend and perfect-order tell you whether an in-line account is still worth keeping.

Density needs colour

Thousands of cells only work if a glance separates in-line, off-track and materially off-track.

The story needs summarising

An AI summary turns a sector of numbers into "what moved, by how much, and why it matters."

04 / Product Tour

A walk through
the suite

From a portfolio read to a single metric's chart, and from a risk score to an exported report - the surfaces that turn a warehouse of data into a decision.

Customer Profile Adherence

Are they using what they committed?

The portfolio read, the sector summary, and the drill-down that ends in a single metric charted against its profile.

Program Health Report

The whole book, colour-coded

Every program on one board - adherence rating, the six profile metrics as a heat-mapped row, and a risk-severity indicator - with a dozen filters to slice by customer, site, sector, storage type or sales owner.

  • Adherence and six metrics per program, at a glance
  • Risk severity beside every row
  • Deep filtering and Excel export
Summary & AI

A sector, read in plain language

The summary dashboard rolls the portfolio into adherence gauges and a sector breakdown by revenue - and an AI summary narrates what moved: which programs shifted off track, how much revenue is exposed, and the sector's average adherence.

  • Adherence gauges: in-line, off-track, materially off
  • Sector breakdown by programs and revenue
  • AI summary that explains the movement
Program detail

Assumption versus reality

Opening a program shows every profile metric against its contracted assumption over trailing 6, 12, 26 and 52-week windows - with a profile-adherence percentage banded by the same colour key used across the suite.

  • Profile assumption vs trailing actuals per metric
  • Four time windows side by side
  • Program summary, months remaining and lost status
Metric deep-dive

Where the drift actually shows

Each metric opens into a profile-versus-actual chart across 52 weeks, with the contracted line held flat against daily actuals and trailing averages - the clearest picture of exactly when an account began to slip.

  • Profile line against daily actual and rolling averages
  • Per-metric tables behind the chart
  • Weekly view, mark-as-active and download
Customer Risk Analysis

Are they profitable, and staying?

The money view - rates versus market, occupancy trend and perfect-order - and the reports the suite hands back.

Risk Analysis

Beyond adherence, to the money

The risk view ranks accounts by revenue and scores each on effective storage and handling rates versus market, occupancy trend year-over-year, and perfect-order - rolling them into a single risk-severity rating.

  • Rates vs market, directional and colour-coded
  • Occupancy trend and perfect-order per account
  • One risk-severity rating per program
Reports

The analysis, delivered

Adherence-and-risk reports are generated per program and land in My Files, each with a processing state - so a commercial manager can pull a Farm Fresh profile-and-risk pack and take it into a customer conversation.

  • Combined profile-adherence + risk reports
  • In-progress, completed and failed states
  • Download, retry and manage from one place
Interaction detail

Reading the dense tables

Hover states across the adherence, program and risk tables surface the number behind each colour without leaving the row.
