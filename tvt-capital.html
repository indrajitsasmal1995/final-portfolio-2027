01 / Requirement

One desk to
evaluate every request

TVT funds small businesses. Every request landed as a pile of documents an underwriter checked by hand across a dozen tabs before pricing anything.

"By the time we'd verified a merchant by hand, the good deals had already taken someone else's offer."- Underwriter, TVT Capital

A funding request carries a business behind it - a website, an EIN, bank statements, a legal entity, an owner. Before anyone could price a deal, every one of those had to be confirmed real, and that confirmation lived in someone's head and browser history.

The brief: put the whole evaluation on one desk - intake, verification, risk and paperwork - so a decision is fast, defensible and never rebuilt from scratch.

01.See every request in one queue

Amount, company, owner, status and assignee on a single board - no chasing a request across inboxes and spreadsheets.

02.Verify the applicant automatically

Website, EIN, phone, address and financials checked and flagged as the request arrives - not eyeballed field by field.

03.Price and paper the deal in place

From a risk score to a priced term sheet to a signature request - one screen, one record, one audit trail.

02 / Research

Following a request
from inbox to signed

Before drawing a screen, I traced how a funding request actually moves - and where it quietly stalls.

11

Underwriting sessions

Shadowing underwriters and ops through live requests, intake to decision.

30+

Deals re-traced

Accepted, rejected and denied requests walked through step by step.

06

Manual checks mapped

The verifications every deal waited on - most done in separate browser tabs.

The sessions kept circling one theme: underwriters weren't slow at judgment - they were slow at trust. Every field had to be independently confirmed before anyone would price a deal, and that confirmation never lived in the product.

That reframed the work: not a prettier CRM, but a desk that does the verifying, turns it into a number, and lets a person make the call.

Trust is the bottleneck

Approval waited on verification, not on judgment. Automate the checks and the decision is minutes, not days.

Risk needs a number

"Gut feel" doesn't scale across a team. A shared score lets any underwriter read a deal the same way.

The record is one object

Request, checks, scores, documents and terms belong on one deal - not scattered across tools.

Every decision leaves a trail

Accept, reject and the reason are captured on the deal, so a call can always be explained later.

04 / Product Tour

A tour of
the desk

The screens that carry a request from a row in a queue to a signed, funded deal - and the supporting tools around them.

The core flow

From queue to signature

Six screens do the real work: the queue, the scored record, the reject state, the pricing panel, the term sheet, and the team around it.

Dashboard

Every request, counted

The desk opens on a scoreboard - total, accepted, denied, rejected, term sheets generated - over a filterable queue of every loan request with its amount, status and assignee.

  • KPI cards summarise the whole pipeline at a glance
  • Filter by date range, status and assignee
  • Owner and status visible on every row
Loan details

Verified fields, scored risk

Open a request and every applicant field carries a green check - website, EIN, phone, address, financials - while the right rail rolls them into a Financial Authenticity Score, a Confidence rating and FICO.

  • Each field independently verified, not eyeballed
  • Three risk signals summarise the applicant
  • Documents and loan terms sit on the same record
Reject state

The reject writes itself

When confidence is low the scores turn amber and red and the reason is stated across the top of the deal - no guessing why a request didn't clear, and a record for later.

  • Reason banner states the decline up front
  • Scores shift colour to signal risk
  • A templated email goes to the applicant
Pricing

Price it in the same panel

The right rail carries the decision through to money: funded amount, rate, term, fee and a pre-pay schedule, with a single button to generate the term sheet from the numbers on screen.

  • Scores and pricing share one panel
  • Rate, term, OG fee and pre-pays in view
  • One click from a priced deal to a term sheet
Term sheet

Generated, then signed

The generated term sheet lays out the placement, cost, remittance and pre-pay terms, with the case metadata beside it - and a single action to send it for signature over DocuSign.

  • Terms generated from the priced deal
  • Borrower, funding and issue details attached
  • Create DocuSign sends it for e-signature
Team & comms

Roles, and the messages that fire

Around the deal sits the operational layer: role-based team access decides who can act, and email templates for accepted, rejected and denied outcomes fire automatically on the decision.

  • Admin and user roles on the team
  • Templated applicant emails per outcome
  • Invites and account management built in
Supporting screens

The rest of the desk

Auth, the term-sheet generation flow, and the team & communications tooling that surround the core loop.
