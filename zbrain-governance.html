01 / Requirement

Agents were shipping
faster than control

ZBrain makes it easy to build agents that act - call tools, touch data, write to systems. At enterprise scale, that autonomy is exactly what has to be governed.

"Every team could ship an agent in a day. Nobody could answer what all of them were allowed to do."- Platform stakeholder, ZBrain

One mis-prompted agent can drop a table or export PII - a mesh of them multiplies the blast radius. The EU AI Act and OWASP Agentic Top 10 demand this be controllable and provable.

The brief: one governance layer over every agent - policy, guardrails, compliance, audit.

01.Set the rules once

Baseline policy templates and custom rules that apply across all solutions and agents, with room to tighten per solution.

02.Block the dangerous by default

A global guardrail library - destructive SQL, shell, PII export, prompt injection - that no agent can override.

03.See and prove everything

Every allowed and blocked action logged, scored for trust, mapped to compliance, and surfaced in a live observatory.

02 / Research

What "governing an
agent" actually means

I worked with the platform and security teams to turn a vague need for "AI governance" into concrete controls an admin could set and defend.

3

Enforcement layers

Policy templates, custom rules and an unoverridable blocked-pattern library.

8

Compliance frameworks

GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 27001, CCPA, OWASP Agentic.

5

Enforcement actions

Allow, warn, throttle, escalate to a human, or deny - per rule.

The insight that shaped the product: governance only works if it's legible - an admin must see what an agent may do, what it just tried, and why it was stopped.

So the design pairs policy with proof: rules on one side - live trust scores, violations and a kill switch on the other.

Autonomy needs a blast radius

A privilege model - root, privileged, standard rings - bounds what each agent can reach before it ever runs.

Trust must be measurable

Every agent carries a trust score that policy violations lower; below threshold, it's automatically suspended.

Compliance is a feature

Toggling a framework auto-populates the policy rules and audit requirements it demands.

Nothing without a trail

Every enforcement decision is logged with the prompt, the rule matched, the action taken and a trace ID.

04 / Product Tour

A walk through
the portal

The observatory that watches, and the governance surfaces that control - templates, guardrails, compliance and the audit trail behind every decision.

The control plane

Watch, then govern

Six surfaces carry an agent from a policy on paper to an enforced, audited decision - starting with the observatory that makes the whole platform legible.

AI Observatory

Health, cost and governance, at a glance

Spend, tokens and pass rate beside live agent health - ring, trust and status - over a running feed of governance events.

  • Agent health with ring, trust and kill switch
  • Governance events: blocks, escalations, trust breaches
  • Meta-harness pass rates across test suites
Governance home

Every policy, agent and violation in one place

Coverage first - how many solutions and agents a policy governs - with agents, violations and policies as tabs.

  • Coverage across solutions and agents
  • Agents, violation logs and policies as tabs
  • Create and manage policies from one board
Policy templates

A baseline, then your own rules

Start from Strict, Balanced or Audit-only, then add custom rules mapping a condition to an action and a scope.

  • Three baseline postures to start from
  • Custom rules: condition → deny, escalate, throttle, warn
  • Inherited account-wide, tightened per solution
Blocked patterns

The lines no agent can cross

Destructive SQL, shell, PII export, prompt injection - regex guardrails no agent or solution can override.

  • Regex guardrails across every agent and solution
  • Categorised and severity-ranked
  • Cannot be overridden at solution or agent level
Violation logs

Why an action was stopped

Each decision opens into the prompt, the rule matched, the action taken, the trust penalty and a trace ID.

  • Prompt, rule matched and enforcement decision
  • Timeline, trust delta and trace ID
  • Reviewer notes and related violations
Compliance frameworks

Regulation, as a toggle

Toggle GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2 or the EU AI Act - each auto-populates the rules and audit requirements it demands.

  • Eight frameworks, on or off per account
  • Each auto-populates rules and audit needs
  • Purpose-built for the EU AI Act & agentic-AI risk
The rest of the portal

Settings, access and the platform it governs
