01 / Requirement

A claim that settles
itself, almost

Personal-injury claims are slow, opaque and paperwork-heavy. Claimants wait months without knowing what their case is worth; firms drown in police reports and medical records.

"People don't want a lawyer's phone number. They want to know what happened to their claim, and what it's worth."- Product lead, CaseSolve

A claim is a pile of evidence - what happened, what was hurt, what it cost. Valuing that pile is slow expert work, and the claimant is left in the dark throughout.

The brief: guide the intake, let AI read the documents, and hand an adjuster a priced case - start to settlement.

01.Guide the claimant

Turn a legal intake into a friendly, branching questionnaire - accident type, injuries, treatment - that anyone can finish in one sitting.

02.Let AI read the evidence

Police reports and medical records get parsed, summarised and categorised automatically, so no one re-types a stack of PDFs.

03.Keep a human on the number

The AI proposes a value; an adjuster reviews every injury, adjusts compensation, and approves or declines the settlement.

Two very different
users, one case

The same case object is lived by a stressed claimant and a busy adjuster. I designed for both without compromising either.

Claimants needed momentum - every screen moving them closer to a number. Adjusters needed the opposite: density, traceability, and the power to override the machine.

So the claimant app hides the complexity behind a stepper; the console exposes all of it, editable.

Momentum for the claimant

A visible 5-step stepper and plain-language questions keep a nervous user moving to the settlement.

Density for the adjuster

Tabs, tables and filters put a whole case on one screen without hiding anything.

AI proposes, human disposes

Every AI output - summary, category, value - is visible, sourced and editable before it counts.

One case, everywhere

The same case object drives the claimant app, the mobile build and the adjuster console.

04 / Product Tour

A walk through
the product

Three surfaces, one design language. Below: the claimant web app, the adjuster console, and the responsive build - screen by screen.

Surface 01 - Claimant Web

Filing a claim, without a lawyer

A friendly, branching intake that turns a legal process into a guided questionnaire - and ends in a plain-language settlement.

Dashboard

Every case, and what it's worth

"Resolve your case in under 30 minutes" - over a status table showing each case and its settlement value.

  • Settlement value visible before a case even closes
  • Colour-coded status: under review, in progress, solved, declined
  • One prominent Get Started path into the flow
Accident intake

The first choice branches the flow

Eight illustrated accident types reroute the entire questionnaire - an MVA and a slip-and-fall need different questions.

  • Illustrated tiles make a dry legal step approachable
  • MVA and Fall drive completely different question sets
  • A 5-step stepper keeps the whole journey in view
AI document analysis

Documents that read themselves

Upload the police report and CaseSolve parses it in place - structured evidence, no re-keying.

  • AI parses the uploaded report automatically
  • Clear success state confirms the analysis is done
  • Feeds straight into the case's evidence record
Injury questions

Point to the pain

Injuries are tapped onto a rotatable 3D body model - the selections drive the follow-up questions.

  • Rotatable model (front and back) for spatial input
  • Selections tailor the treatment and pain questions
  • A video tutorial precedes it for first-timers
Records & payment

Order records without leaving the case

No records on hand? CaseSolve fetches them - Stripe checkout embedded in the flow, so the case never stalls.

  • Embedded Stripe payment, itemised and taxed
  • CaseSolve retrieves records on the claimant's behalf
  • Or upload existing records directly instead
Settlement report

A settlement you can actually read

An AI-drafted report retells the accident, summarises the injuries and states the award - in plain language.

  • Evidence, accident, injuries and award, sectioned clearly
  • Every source document listed for transparency
  • Binding award with a stated 24-hour payout
Surface 02 - Admin Console

Where a human sets the number

The adjuster's workspace: a queue of cases, a tabbed case file, AI-generated medical summaries, and an editable settlement.

Settlements queue

A queue built for adjusters

Every case lands with its type, proposed value, action items and status - triage at a glance.

  • Status pills: needs review, approved, declined, in progress
  • Action-item count flags cases waiting on a human
  • Search and filter by status and accident type
AI medical summaries

The AI reads the medical records

A stack of medical records becomes a searchable table - summarised, dated, and linked to the source PDF.

  • Every summary links its source document
  • Auto-categorised: doctor visits, physical therapy, surgery
  • Adjuster notes editable on each row
Settlement

Numbers a human can adjust

The AI proposes compensation per injury; the adjuster edits any line and the case value recalculates.

  • Per-injury compensation, fully editable
  • Deductions and total case value roll up live
  • One-click report download for the claimant
Surface 03 - Responsive

The same case, in a pocket

The claimant flow condensed for mobile - the stepper collapses to icons, the tables scroll, nothing is lost.
