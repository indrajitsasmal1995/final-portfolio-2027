How it works

Three taps a board.

Recorded between boards, while the coins are being set, not pieced together after the game.

  1. 1

    Pick the four on the board

    Two a side, picked fresh for every board.

  2. 2

    Tap the side that won

    One tap. Each side keeps its own colour everywhere in the app.

  3. 3

    Slide the points, add the board

    Every phone updates at once. First side to 29 takes the game.

Got a board wrong last week? Fix it, and the whole game re-scores itself.
